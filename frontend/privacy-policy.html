Privacy Policy

How SnapEats uses customer and platform data to deliver, support, and improve the service.

Privacy on SnapEats is about using the information necessary to complete orders, support accounts, reduce fraud, and improve the product while keeping the scope tied to real service needs. The goal is operational usefulness, not unnecessary data collection.

What We Collect

The information collected should map to actual platform needs.

  • Basic account details like name, phone, email, and saved addresses.
  • Order history, restaurant selections, and transaction-linked context.
  • Technical or device-level information needed for performance, security, or troubleshooting.

How It Is Used

Data supports fulfillment, support, safety, and product quality.

Operational use
  • Delivering orders and enabling customer support.
  • Helping merchants and delivery partners complete the request.
  • Handling account security, issue review, and fraud detection.
Improvement use
  • Understanding product friction and experience quality.
  • Reviewing failures, delays, and recurring support patterns.
  • Improving checkout, discovery, and service stability over time.

Questions And Requests

Users should have a clear way to ask about account-related information.

If you need help understanding stored information, correcting account details, or making a privacy-related request, contact support with the email or phone tied to the affected account so the team can review it properly.

Ask a privacy question
Privacy is closely tied to support, fulfillment, and security. The more clearly a request identifies the affected account, the easier it is to review responsibly.
