Platform rules that keep ordering, accounts, and service behavior fair and workable.

These terms explain the broad responsibilities that apply when using SnapEats. They are meant to set practical expectations for customers, restaurants, and platform operators around ordering, account use, service limits, and misuse prevention.

Use Rules

The service depends on accurate details, fair behavior, and realistic expectations.

  • Users should provide correct contact, delivery, and payment information.
  • Restaurants should maintain accurate menus, pricing, and availability details.
  • All parties should avoid fraudulent behavior, abuse, or attempts to manipulate offers and refunds.

Orders And Service

Every order is still subject to acceptance, serviceability, and live operational conditions.

What affects an order
  • Restaurant acceptance and item availability.
  • Delivery zone coverage and partner availability.
  • Campaign windows, fees, and checkout conditions.
What users should understand
  • Pricing and delivery-related details can vary by restaurant and city.
  • Some orders cannot be cancelled after preparation begins.
  • Operational issues can change timing even after an order is accepted.

Accounts And Misuse

Accounts may be limited when platform trust is being abused.

SnapEats may investigate and act on behavior such as repeated fraudulent refunds, false claims, abusive communication, or attempts to exploit pricing and promotional systems. Account actions are intended to protect customers, merchants, riders, and the service overall.
