Service Overview

Built for neighborhood convenience when speed and clarity both matter.

Genie supports use cases like small package movements, document runs, everyday pickups, and convenience-led errands. The service is best suited to lightweight local tasks that can be fulfilled with clear addresses and responsive contacts on both ends.

Simple request types

Ideal for short-distance pickup and drop requests that do not require complex handling.

Operational guardrails

Serviceability, item rules, and rider availability keep expectations realistic.

How It Works

A clear pickup, route, and delivery flow is what makes Genie useful.

Pickup stage
  • Customers share item details, landmarks, and the best contact information.
  • Pickup readiness matters because Genie requests are often time-sensitive.
  • Clear handoff instructions reduce delays and failed attempts.
Drop stage
  • Recipients should stay reachable during the delivery window.
  • Address clarity helps riders avoid unnecessary reroutes.
  • Updates are most useful when both pickup and drop contacts respond quickly.

Guidelines

The service works best when item type, packaging, and handoff details are clear from the start.

  • Package lightweight items securely before pickup.
  • Avoid restricted, fragile, or unclear items without prior confirmation.
  • Keep both parties reachable until delivery is completed.
