Refund Policy

How SnapEats reviews refund requests when orders do not go as promised.

Refund decisions depend on what happened, when it was reported, and whether the issue can still be resolved in another way. The platform aims to review valid cases fairly while also keeping the process grounded in order evidence and timing.

Common Eligible Cases

Refund review usually starts with whether the order failed in a meaningful, verifiable way.

  • Orders accepted but not delivered.
  • Items missing, clearly wrong, or materially different from what was ordered.
  • Payments processed for orders that could not be fulfilled.
  • Certain cancellations within the allowed window and policy rules.

Review Factors

Context matters when deciding whether a case gets a full refund, partial refund, or another resolution path.

What helps a claim
  • Fast reporting after delivery or failed service.
  • Order IDs, screenshots, or photos where relevant.
  • Clear descriptions of what went wrong.
Possible outcomes
  • Full refund where the order failed materially.
  • Partial refund when only part of the order was affected.
  • Credit or alternative support when another resolution is more appropriate.

Timelines

Payment mode and review complexity can affect how quickly the money reflects.

Eligible refunds are generally initiated after review and often reflect within a few business days depending on the payment instrument used. The clearest way to avoid delay is to report the issue quickly with the right context.
