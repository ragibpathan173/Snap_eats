Become A Rider

Onboarding should feel practical, not confusing.

Riders typically need identity and vehicle documentation, a reliable device, and readiness to work across local delivery conditions. City availability can differ based on demand, service zones, and operational readiness.

  • Application pathways are city-dependent and may open in phases.
  • Riders may support food deliveries and selected local convenience tasks.
  • Route quality improves when pickup and drop details are clearly captured in-app.

Earnings Model

A dependable earning experience depends on more than just trip volume.

Common earning inputs
  • Completed trip count and route distance.
  • Time-of-day incentives and demand-linked opportunities.
  • Operational efficiency and successful handoff completion.
What helps riders most
  • Accurate pickup readiness from merchants.
  • Clear customer instructions and reachable contacts.
  • Support channels that resolve confusion quickly.

Safety And Support

Support should show up when the trip gets messy, not after it is already over.

Riders need a clear path for escalation during address issues, unsafe situations, payout questions, and customer handoff problems. Safety reports and unusual situations should always be raised quickly so operations teams can respond with the right urgency.
