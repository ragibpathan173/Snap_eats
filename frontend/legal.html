Terms & Conditions

Using SnapEats means agreeing to fair use, accurate information, and respectful platform behavior.

Customers are expected to provide correct contact, payment, and delivery details; merchants are expected to maintain accurate menus and operating information; and all users must avoid fraudulent use, abusive behavior, or attempts to manipulate pricing, offers, or refunds.

  • Orders are subject to restaurant acceptance, serviceability, and availability.
  • Prices, fees, and offers may vary by restaurant, city, and active promotion.
  • SnapEats may limit or suspend accounts involved in misuse, fraud, or repeated policy violations.

Cookie Policy

Cookies help the platform remember preferences, improve sessions, and measure product quality.

SnapEats uses cookies and similar storage tools to keep users signed in where appropriate, remember essential settings, understand feature usage, and maintain performance across sessions. Some cookies are necessary for the service to work, while others support analytics and product improvement.

  • Essential use: session continuity, security checks, and core navigation.
  • Preference use: remembering recent choices like city, language, or interface settings.
  • Performance use: understanding which flows are slow, confusing, or failing.

Privacy Policy

We collect the data needed to run the service and improve it, not more than the experience requires.

Information such as names, addresses, phone numbers, order history, payment references, and device-level technical data may be processed to fulfill orders, support accounts, investigate issues, prevent fraud, and improve the product. Data is handled with access controls and operational safeguards appropriate to the platform.

  • Operational teams use data to deliver orders, resolve issues, and maintain account security.
  • Limited sharing may occur with merchants, delivery partners, and service providers when needed to complete a request.
  • Users can contact support for questions about stored information, corrections, or account-related requests.

Refund Policy

Refund outcomes depend on what went wrong, what was delivered, and whether the order can still be recovered.

Refunds are usually reviewed for issues such as failed payments, unavailable items, order cancellations within policy, materially incorrect deliveries, or verified service failures. Some cases may qualify for a full refund, while others may be resolved through a partial refund, wallet credit, or replacement support.

Situations often eligible for review
  • Order accepted but not delivered.
  • Items missing or clearly different from what was ordered.
  • Payments processed for orders that could not be fulfilled.
What speeds up a decision
  • Quick reporting after delivery or cancellation.
  • Photos or screenshots where relevant.
  • Accurate order IDs and a concise issue description.
