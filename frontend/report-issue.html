Report An Issue

A dedicated page for order problems, app bugs, billing concerns, and urgent service complaints.

Some issues need a clearer route than general support. This page is for reporting things like broken flows, severe service failures, safety concerns, menu mismatches, or anything else that should be reviewed with stronger urgency and better evidence.

What To Report

Use this route when the issue is serious, recurring, or not being resolved properly elsewhere.

  • Wrong, missing, or misleading menu information.
  • Severe order problems that were not handled well the first time.
  • Payment anomalies, coupon failures, or checkout bugs.
  • Safety concerns, harassment, or suspicious platform behavior.

How To Share It

Good issue reports are short, specific, and supported by the right details.

What helps immediately
  • Order ID, account phone number, and city.
  • Screenshots, timestamps, and relevant payment references.
  • A concise summary of what happened and what should have happened instead.
What slows review down
  • Missing order context or no evidence when evidence exists.
  • Long descriptions that never state the main problem clearly.
  • Reporting too late after the issue when the order was still active earlier.

Escalation Path

Urgent issues should be routed fast to the right support lane.

If the issue touches safety, fraud, repeated severe failure, or an unresolved live order concern, it should be clearly labeled in the first report so the support team can escalate it correctly.
