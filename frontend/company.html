About Us

A product shaped around everyday food decisions, not just transactions.

SnapEats started with a simple idea: ordering food should feel calm and clear. We designed the experience around practical needs like transparent delivery fees, trustworthy restaurant discovery, repeat-order convenience, and support that does not disappear after checkout.

Discovery that respects time

Useful filters, strong restaurant cards, and fewer dead ends when someone just wants dinner fast.

Operational clarity

Menu data, checkout, and order tracking are built to reduce confusion for customers and merchants alike.

Long-term trust

We aim for consistent service, not one-time spikes. Retention matters more than noisy growth.

Team

A compact team balancing product craft with real-world delivery execution.

SnapEats is built by a cross-functional group that blends engineering, growth, merchant partnerships, and rider operations. We work close to the surface area of the business so product decisions stay grounded in what restaurants, customers, and support teams actually face every day.

  • Product and engineering: fast browsing, dependable checkout, and friction-free account flows.
  • Merchant success: menu quality, pricing clarity, and better repeat conversion.
  • Operations: delivery windows, issue response, and local fulfillment quality.
  • Brand and community: storytelling, launches, and city-level campaigns.

SnapEats One

A membership designed around repeat value, not gimmicks.

SnapEats One gives frequent customers a better everyday experience with lower effective delivery costs, member-only offers, priority support lanes, and cleaner reorder flows. The aim is simple: make regular ordering feel worth it without hiding the value behind complicated rules.

  • Lower delivery charges on eligible orders.
  • Member-first drops and occasional partner offers.
  • Faster support routing when plans or orders need attention.

SnapEats Genie

Quick errands and on-demand pickups for the moments food delivery is not enough.

SnapEats Genie extends the platform into lighter local logistics. Customers can use it for pickup-and-drop requests, urgent small-item deliveries, and convenience runs within supported service zones. It is built for reliability, address clarity, and realistic fulfillment promises.

  • Best suited for short-distance deliveries and lightweight parcel requests.
  • Serviceability depends on local rider availability and item restrictions.
  • Live status updates help customers stay aligned on ETA and handoff.
