Cookie Policy

How browser storage and cookies help the SnapEats experience stay usable and consistent.

Cookies and similar storage tools help SnapEats remember core session details, keep the experience consistent, and understand how the product is performing. Some are essential to the service, while others support convenience and improvement.

Session continuity Preference memory Experience improvement
Essential use Preference use Control and questions

Essential Use

Some storage is necessary for the service to function at all.

  • Maintaining a usable session while you move through the ordering flow.
  • Supporting account state, security checks, and sign-in continuity.
  • Remembering the basic context needed for pages and interactions to work properly.

Preference Use

Other storage helps the experience feel more personal and less repetitive.

Examples of remembered preferences
  • Location or delivery context.
  • Interface state and practical convenience choices.
  • Settings that help reduce repetitive inputs across visits.
Why that matters
  • It reduces friction during repeat use.
  • It helps pages feel more consistent from visit to visit.
  • It can support a more relevant ordering experience.

Control And Questions

Users can manage browser behavior, but some controls may affect how well the service works.

Blocking or clearing cookies can affect session continuity, saved preferences, and the stability of parts of the ordering flow. If you have questions about how storage supports the experience, contact support and describe the issue you are seeing.
